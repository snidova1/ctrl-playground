# Key-holder revenue sharing — tentative product intent, NOT implemented

User direction: Activities exist so players have things to do. Do not promise financial rewards for gardening, fishing, cooking, crafting, contributing or barter.

Potential future revenue source: an allocation from Key trading fees. Not a token emission or gameplay payout commitment.

Weight interpretation acknowledged to owner:
```
weight(holder) = sum(Key balances held for creators other than holder)
```
- Count Key units, not distinct creators.
- An equal total quantity concentrated in one other creator or spread over several other creators has equal weight.
- Own creator Keys do not count.
- Holding another creator's Key confers no edit rights in that creator's Habitat.

Potential proportional distribution (proposal only): allocated fee pool × holder weight / sum of eligible holder weights. Fee-pool allocation, eligible markets/holders, snapshot time or time weighting, rounding, own-identity handling and settlement mechanism are not finalized. No contract, payout claim, financial eligibility badge or reward balance is implemented by the cozy-life work.

Cozy-life items are nonfinancial game items; gameplay inventory and community contribution totals must not be presented as revenue-share weights.
